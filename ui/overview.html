The office

A person for every node, as busy as the node. Trouble shows as litter, then cracks; the floor only turns to lava when things are really bad.

Opening the office…

Control planes sit in the glass room; workers on the open floor. Hover a person for their node, click to open it. Blue screens are nodes that are down, smoking screens nodes with problems, the couch is for cordoned ones, boxes by the door are pods no node has taken, the server racks are the nodes four to a rack, and the hooded figures are the security findings.
